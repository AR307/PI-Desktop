# PI Android 0.20.3

## Scope

This is an Android-only release from published 0.20.2 (`ebf4d060a`). Workspace
versions are synchronized to 0.20.3. The Windows desktop stays at its published
0.20.2 packages, and no desktop assets or update feed are published. The
Android APK and its `mobile-update.json` are published to
`AR307/Mirrorcoding-APP`. The source tag `v0.20.3` is in `AR307/PI-Desktop`.

## Changes since 0.20.2

- ColorOS, OriginOS, HyperOS and EMUI/MagicOS can freeze the app and its
  WebView in the background despite the foreground service. Attention then
  waits in the open socket until the app is opened. The phone now records late
  delivery: an attention arrives 90 s or more after the event, the
  desktop-to-phone clock offset is measured with `connection/ping`, keep-alive
  was running, and the attention is handled while hidden or right after
  returning. Sessions and Activity then show the vendor's background switch.
  ADR mobile-attention-push records the finding in its 2026-10-15 amendment.
- Vendors known to freeze get the guidance up front in Activity. The notice
  and Me can request Android's battery-optimization exemption
  (`REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS`, distributed outside Google Play).
- Returning from a system dialog refreshes notification and battery state.
